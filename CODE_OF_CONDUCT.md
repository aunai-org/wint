# Code of conduct

wint is a small project and we want it to be a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and contribute.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, especially from people who are new to the field or to Rust. Many users here are learning.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.
- Don't share other people's private information.
- Keep it on topic and constructive. A clear, patient answer helps more than a clever one.

## What happens if it isn't followed

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who keep breaking these rules, without warning for serious cases.

## Reporting

If someone is behaving badly, or you were treated badly, tell the maintainers privately instead of replying in public. Use GitHub's private reporting: on this repository open the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say it is a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential.

This applies to the `wint` and `wint-demo` repositories.
